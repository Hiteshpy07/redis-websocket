Privacy Policy for Co-Sketch

Effective Date: March 2026

Co-Sketch ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our Chrome Extension handles user data.

1. Information We Collect

Co-Sketch collects only the minimal data strictly necessary to provide real-time collaborative sketching and whiteboard services:

  • Account & Profile Information: When signing in via Google or GitHub OAuth, we receive your public name, email address, and avatar image to display your identity in collaborative sketch rooms.
  • Session Data: Temporary authentication tokens stored locally via Chrome Storage to keep you signed in across browser sessions.
  • Collaboration Data: Canvas drawing coordinates, shape properties, and room chat messages transmitted strictly within your chosen room ID via secure WebSockets.

2. What We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not track or monitor your web browsing history, search queries, or visited URLs.
  • We do not read, modify, or inspect content on any third-party websites you visit.
  • We do not collect financial, medical, or precise geolocation information.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you and display your handle/avatar to other participants in your sketch room.
  • Synchronize real-time strokes and messages between room participants using WebSockets and Redis.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or advertisers. Data is only transmitted to our secure backend servers to power the real-time canvas features.

5. Security

All communication between the extension and backend services is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS & WSS).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via GitHub: https://github.com/Hiteshpy07/redis-websocket
